The Bigger Picture
Three layers. Each one makes the last one worth more.
The 3 Visit Diagnostic already lives in layer one. Layers two and three are where the same retention story turns into a commercial one, and where the conversation with an operator stops being about covers and starts being about profit.
Layer01

SevenRooms only: the retention picture

Source: SevenRooms API · booking, tag and guest-profile data

Everything the diagnostic does today, but live and self-updating rather than a point-in-time review. Visit 1 → 2 → 3 progression, drop-off by site, by day part, by server, by booking channel. Cancellation and no-show patterns. Lapsed regulars flagged the week they go quiet, not the quarter after.

"What share of December's first-time guests came back within 90 days, by site?"
"Which regulars haven't booked in 60 days but used to come monthly?"
Layer02

Add the POS and the marketing spend: retention with a price tag

Adds: POS / EPOS · email & SMS platform · Meta & Google Ads · Google Analytics

Booking data tells you a guest returned. Spend data tells you what they were worth when they did. Join them and you can put a number on the 5%: actual revenue per retained guest, second-visit spend versus first, and which campaigns bring back guests who stick versus guests who take the offer and vanish. The ROI case you already argue, evidenced from the operator's own numbers.

"What's the average lifetime spend of a guest who reaches visit 3 versus one who stops at visit 1?"
"Which re-engagement campaign produced returning guests, and which just discounted people who were coming anyway?"
Layer03

Add finance, labour and supply: the whole operator

Adds: Xero / Sage · rota & labour · stock and purchasing · HR

Now retention sits inside the P&L. You can show that the sites with the strongest visit-3 conversion are also the ones with the lowest staff churn, or that a drop in return rate tracks a specific manager change or a rota pattern. This is the layer that moves the conversation from a retention project to a board-level one, and it's usually where the engagement stops being a one-off diagnostic.

"Do sites with lower team turnover convert more guests to visit 3?"
"What did the retention lift add to gross profit per site this quarter, net of the labour cost to deliver it?"
